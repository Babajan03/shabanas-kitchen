A big steel vessel, a family recipe and neighbours who kept asking for one more jar.

Shabana's Kitchen started the way most good food does: at home, for people we love. A batch of avakaya for the family became a batch for the street, and soon the street was calling with orders.

Today we still cook the same way. Mangoes are cut and cured by hand, chillies are dried and ground in-house, and every jar is filled while the masala is still fragrant. When you open one, it should smell like a kitchen in Andhra, not a warehouse.

  • Recipes tuned to Rayalaseema and coastal Andhra tastes
  • Salt, oil, sun and spice do the preserving
  • Made in lots small enough to taste every batch ourselves

Shabana

From market basket to your kitchen shelf in four unhurried steps.

1

Pick the best

Firm raw mangoes, tender gongura, ripe tomatoes and fresh meat, chosen by hand from local growers and markets.

2

Sun-dry and cure

Cut pieces rest under the Andhra sun with salt and turmeric until they are ready to take on the masala.

3

Grind and mix

Chilli, mustard, fenugreek and garlic are roasted and ground in small lots, then folded through warm oil.

4

Jar, seal and ship

Jars are filled, sealed tight, double-wrapped and handed to the courier so they reach you leak-free.
